ⓘ How the week works
A week runs Sunday to Saturday. Plan my week fills the empty nights by your answers; + Add puts one dinner on a day, or open any recipe under Recipes and add it from there. Tap a dinner to open it, swap it, cook it double or take it off. Move between weeks with the arrows or a swipe; the week you are looking at is yours, and the plan itself is shared with your household. Days gone by keep what was planned and how you rated it. The list at the foot is for the week on screen.
